# KinderDraw Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2 September 2026**

KinderDraw is a drawing app for young children. It is free, contains no
advertising, and is open source under the MIT licence.

## The short version

KinderDraw collects nothing, sends nothing, and asks for nothing. It has no
account, no login, and no way to reach the internet at all. Your child's
drawings stay on your device.

## What the app stores, and where

When a drawing is saved, it is written to your device's own picture storage,
in a KinderDraw album alongside your other photo albums. That is the only
thing the app saves.

Those drawings are ordinary picture files on your device. They show up in your
gallery or photos app like any other picture, and you can view, share, or
delete them from there. Because they are normal pictures in your device's
picture storage, whatever backup or sync you already use for your photos — a
cloud photo service, for instance — will treat them the way it treats your
other albums. That is your device and your photo app's behaviour, under your
control and your existing settings, not something KinderDraw does or can
influence.

KinderDraw does not read any of your other photos, and does not touch pictures
it did not create.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect personal information of any kind.
- It does not transmit anything off your device. The app requests **no
  internet permission**, so it is not technically capable of sending data
  anywhere, to us or to anyone else.
- It contains no advertising, and no advertising software.
- It contains no analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting software.
- It does not use an advertising identifier.
- It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no user profiles.
- It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

## Children's privacy

KinderDraw is intended for young children, and is designed accordingly. It
collects no personal information from anyone, including children. There is no
mechanism in the app by which a child could disclose personal information,
because there is nothing in it that sends information anywhere and no way to
type or share anything from within it.

There are no advertisements, no in-app purchases, no external links, and no
social features.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and the
date at the top will change. Because the app is open source, the full history
of this document is public, and any change can be inspected alongside the code
change that prompted it.
